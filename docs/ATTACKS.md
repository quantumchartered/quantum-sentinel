## Limits of this scoreboard

- It tests Sentinel's own controls. It cannot show how real banks or the real Airwallex sandbox behave; those runs are pending keys.
- The AI attacks use scripted "compromised" models that do exactly what the injected text asks. That is a worst case for obedience, but it is not a measurement of a real model's behaviour.
- The audit-tamper attack assumes a database superuser. The hash chain detects the edit, but it cannot prevent a superuser from rewriting the whole chain or deleting its newest entries. Exported checkpoints (T5) narrow that gap.
